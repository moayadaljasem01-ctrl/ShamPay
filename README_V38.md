# ShamPay v38 — Fintech Experience Suite (DEMO)

- Home quick actions open glass bottom sheets. Sending uses the existing verified recipient → review → OTP + PIN flow; receiving creates a local fictional transaction.
- Transaction ledger, balances, receipts, and notifications remain synchronized via the existing `complete()` flow.
- Notification bell opens a glass panel; individual read/delete and read-all/clear-all supported.
- Existing persisted light/dark themes and Arabic/English switching retained; glass surfaces themed.
- Center QR tab opens a QR hub with My QR / Scanner tabs; QR image save, share, clipboard, optional camera and manual fallback.
- **No real money, licensed payment processing, or identity verification.** All records are local demo data. Do not enter real personal data.
- QA: verify on iPhone Safari and installed PWA; camera decoding depends on browser support.
